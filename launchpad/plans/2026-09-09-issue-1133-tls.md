  **The relay's only in-process TLS signal is `RELAY_URL`'s scheme.**
  `crates/buzz-relay/src/config.rs` loads it as
  `std::env::var("RELAY_URL").unwrap_or_else(|_| "ws://localhost:3000".to_string())`
  with **no** scheme validation — in direct contrast to `BUZZ_PAIRING_RELAY_URL`
  three lines below, which parses and rejects anything that is not `ws`/`wss`.
  That scheme is consumed by exactly two auth helpers in
  `crates/buzz-relay/src/api/bridge.rs`: `nip42_expected_relay_url` (`wss://` prefix →
  `wss`, else `ws`) and `nip98_expected_url` (`wss://` prefix → `https`, else `http`),
  plus `relay_url_for_tenant_host` in `crates/buzz-relay/src/api/nip05.rs`.
  **A scheme mismatch fails NIP-42 AUTH, verified rather than inherited from a sibling
  agent's report.** `crates/buzz-auth/src/nip42.rs`'s private `normalize_relay_url`
  folds `localhost`/`::1` to `127.0.0.1` and strips a trailing slash, and does
  **nothing** to the scheme; `verify_nip42_event` then returns
  `AuthError::RelayUrlMismatch` when
  `normalize_relay_url(relay) != normalize_relay_url(relay_url)`. So a `wss://`-fronted
  deployment left on the `ws://` default rejects every client that signs the URL it
  actually connected to.
