## The demonstration page

Every personal name below is a **synthetic placeholder**, invented for this
demonstration and stated as such in the page itself. None is a real person and none is
drawn from any real roster, contributor list or access-control system.

```markdown
## Who can approve a release

Documented by Alex Example — and release approval is restricted to Taylor Sample.

## Who can approve a deploy

The deploy allowlist is Jordan Placeholder.
```

Line 11 deliberately carries **both roles on one line**: an attribution ("Documented
by …") and an access-control entry ("release approval is restricted to …"). That is the
case the column offsets exist for, and it is the case a line number alone cannot resolve.

**Candidates in: 3.**

## 2. Each candidate's own invocation record

The dispatch *input* necessarily carries the name and its sentence. The dispatch *record*
must not: `screen-sensitive/SKILL.md` forbids a flagged span surviving into a log or a
tool call's arguments, and recording it would breach the very rule this step exists to
demonstrate. Each record below therefore identifies its candidate **by line and offsets
and by the returned verdict, and by nothing else**.

```json
{ "candidate": 1, "line": 11, "col_start": 14, "col_end": 26,
  "verifier": "configured verifier", "verdict": "ATTRIBUTION", "parse_failure": null,
  "outcome": "dropped from the combined outcome (protection removed)" }

{ "candidate": 2, "line": 11, "col_start": 67, "col_end": 80,
  "verifier": "configured verifier", "verdict": "ROSTER_DATA", "parse_failure": null,
  "outcome": "retains redact" }

{ "candidate": 3, "line": 15, "col_start": 24, "col_end": 42,
  "verifier": "deliberately unresolvable", "verdict": null,
  "parse_failure": "2b: line two \"…\" is not equal to any verdict literal",
  "outcome": "retains redact" }
```

Checked rather than asserted: the records were grepped for every placeholder name and
every given name in isolation. **All absent.**

## 3. Reconciliation

```
candidates in:         3
candidates resolved:   3   (each reached a verdict or a named failure)
in the final outcome:  2   [(11, 67, 80), (15, 24, 42)]
dropped (ATTRIBUTION): 1   [(11, 14, 26)]
```

**Nothing was silently dropped, and nothing was left unaccounted for.** Every candidate
that entered is accounted for on exactly one side of that split.

## 4. What the two same-line candidates prove

Candidate 1 and candidate 2 sit on **one line**, differ **only** in their column offsets,
and were resolved to **opposite outcomes** — one dropped, one kept. Under the pre-STEP-7
finding shape they carried a line number and `match: null`, making them indistinguishable;
applying candidate 1's `ATTRIBUTION` verdict to candidate 2 would have removed protection
from an access-control entry, on the one category that exists to stop exactly that.

This is the failure the offsets were added to prevent, demonstrated rather than argued.

## 5. The unresolvable candidate retains protection

Candidate 3 was pointed at a verifier that runs to completion and returns prose carrying
no recognisable verdict. It produced a §2b parse failure — **and kept its `redact`**.

This is the important direction. `screen-sensitive` states that **only** a dispatch that
both completed and parsed cleanly and returned `ATTRIBUTION` for that exact candidate may
remove protection; every other outcome retains the underlying `redact` and stays in the
combined outcome. An unresolved candidate is not reported separately and is not dropped —
that would be #2110's undefined-consumer-action fail-open reappearing at the skill layer,
where the script's retained `redact` cannot reach it.

## 6. The script's own floor is untouched

`screen-content`'s raw output for all three candidates: `disposition` is `redact`,
`requires_dispatch` is `true`, `match` is `null`. The dispatch resolution happens entirely
at the skill layer. **A consumer reading this JSON directly — CI, a script, the scheduled
workflow — never dispatches and still sees three `redact` findings**, which is the
fail-closed floor #2110 established and this Feature was required to preserve rather than
replace.
